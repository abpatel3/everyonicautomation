Our Services

01 LOGIC & LADDER PROGRAMMING

PLC Automation & Logic Development

We design and implement custom PLC programs, structured ladder logic, machine sequencing routines, and fail-safe safety interlocks tailored to your mechanical operations.

  • Multi-brand PLC integration (Siemens, Delta, Schneider, Mitsubishi)
  • Motion control, servo synchronization, and precise positioning
  • Comprehensive logic debugging, simulation, and on-site testing
02 CUSTOM DESIGN & ASSEMBLY

Control Panel Manufacturing

Engineered control panels built to strict industrial standards with neat cable dressing, precise component ratings, thermal calculations, and complete circuit verification.

  • Custom VFD panels, APFC panels, and MCC / PCC assemblies
  • IP-rated enclosures with forced cooling and dust protection
  • 100% pre-dispatch factory testing and continuity verification
03 TURNKEY INTEGRATION

Plant & Process Automation

Complete process automation spanning sensors, transmitters, automated valves, network communication, and plant-level central supervisory control.

  • Continuous batch control, temperature profiling, and flow regulation
  • Industrial Ethernet, Modbus, Profinet, and RS485 telemetry
  • SCADA & HMI operator dashboards with historical alarm logging
04 HMI & SUPERVISORY CONTROL

SCADA & HMI Development

Interactive SCADA graphics, multi-touch HMI operator screens, real-time trend charts, and centralized supervisory dashboards for plant visibility.

  • Real-time process monitoring, historical data logging & alarm management
  • Custom HMI screens for Siemens, Delta, Allen-Bradley & Pro-face screens
  • Remote web dashboards and secure Industrial IoT telemetry integration
05 FIELD SENSORS & TELEMETRY

Process Instrument Selection & Installation

End-to-end selection, field mounting, cable tray laying, shielding termination, and loop calibration for industrial transmitters and sensors.

  • Pressure, temperature, level, and electromagnetic flow meters
  • Cable tray installation, signal wire shielding, and marshalling termination
  • On-site loop testing, transmitter zero-span calibration, and signal verification
06 UTILITY & ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL

Water Treatment & Pumping Automation

Automated control systems for Water Treatment Plants (WTP), Sewage Treatment Plants (STP), ETP, pumping stations, and water booster house facilities.

  • Chemical dosing, filtration sequencing, and automatic backwash control
  • Pumping station telemetry, sump level interlocks, and pump auto-alternation
  • Municipal and industrial water SCADA integration with remote web monitoring
07 SPECIAL PURPOSE MACHINERY

Pharma, Ceramic & SPM Machine Control

High-speed machine automation tailored for pharmaceutical labelling/tablet/filling machines, ceramic tile assembly lines, and Special Purpose Machinery (SPM).

  • Multi-axis servo motion synchronization for precise high-speed indexing
  • Custom machine logic for pharmaceutical packaging and liquid filling lines
  • Integrated safety light curtains, vision sensor triggers, and fault diagnostics
08 END-TO-END EXECUTION

System Integration & On-Site Commissioning

Complete turnkey project execution spanning electrical CAD schematics, panel wiring diagrams, site installation, loop checking, trial runs, and operator handover.

  • CAD electrical wiring schematics, I/O mapping, and FAT / SAT testing
  • Field-proven automation engineers deployed on-site for rapid startup
  • Comprehensive operator training, maintenance manuals, and ongoing support
