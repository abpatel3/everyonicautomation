Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026 | Effective for Everyonic Automation Industrial Services

1. Overview & Commitment

Everyonic Automation ("we", "our", or "us") respects the confidentiality and privacy of our clients, suppliers, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information collected through our website and industrial engineering communications.

2. Information Collection

We collect information you directly provide to us when requesting technical consultations, RFQs, or support services. This may include your name, business email, telephone number, company name, and technical project parameters.

3. Use of Technical Data

Information collected is strictly utilized to prepare engineering quotations, deliver control systems, coordinate on-site automation installations, and provide ongoing maintenance support. We never sell or share client data with third parties.

4. Contact Information

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please email info@everyonicautomation.in or write to Everyonic Automation, Morbi, Gujarat, India.
